# Commercial strategy hypotheses

This is a planning note, not a price commitment or legal offer. The product should preserve these options without putting prices or fictional subscription entitlements into V0.1 code.

- Free player participation.
- Free base GM tier.
- Expanded GM tier (working name “Master”), preliminary hypothesis around USD 7/month.
- Creator/professional tier, preliminary hypothesis around USD 15/month.
- Possible self-hosted server distribution.
- Original campaign/content sales and a future Marketplace commission.

Validate willingness to pay, hosting and storage costs, taxes, regional availability, and creator economics before launch. Keep entitlements configurable and separate from campaign/domain rules. V0.1 has no payment provider, payment data collection, or artificial feature limits. The owner must decide commercial terms, source licensing, and distribution rights.
