# Dark Tavern RPG design vision

**Status** `PLANNED` · Concept for a later visual milestone.

The visual language is minimalist dark fantasy with the atmosphere of a medieval tavern. Dark wood, worn leather, restrained metal and muted bronze belong in the chrome. Keep edge treatments and texture low contrast so that alerts, permissions and game pieces stay readable. The map remains the center of the tabletop and encounter interfaces. Open books, grimoires and scrolls give reference materials a consistent presentation without making ordinary navigation rely on decorative imagery.

Token designs use a small, high-contrast silhouette, class or creature symbols, or a crop of an original or licensed portrait. They stay distinguishable at the minimum tabletop zoom and by more than color. Source and rights metadata must follow the asset. Use optimized CSS, SVG and appropriately sized images. Avoid loading full-resolution art for thumbnails and keep art on a separate budget from the map.

GM and Player interfaces have different actions and information density, with the same visual language. GM preparation, secrets, adjudication and delivery controls stay on the relevant work surfaces. Players see the map and permitted actions. Theme tokens, asset selection and decorative effects sit outside game logic and permission checks. Animations are short, optional and respect reduced motion. Provide a control to turn off decoration, especially texture overlays, shadows and movement.

Plan visual delivery after the V0.1.1 gate as an owner-reviewed increment coordinated with the V0.2 tabletop work. Establish map legibility, keyboard focus, contrast, narrow viewport controls and rendering budgets with both roles before rolling the theme into later reference and authoring modules.
