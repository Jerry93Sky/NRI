# Amber Crossing: The Lantern Vault — Play Guide

**Status:** playable demo content; run `pnpm db:seed:playable-amber` after migrations. The seed is additive and idempotent. It creates a separate campaign and leaves the existing Amber Crossing campaign untouched.

## Session outline (30–60 minutes)

This rules-neutral mystery works with any tabletop system. The GM can map Resolve to a stress/effort track and Wounds to harm, or rename both resources to match the table’s rules.

1. **The Copper Kettle (5–10 min).** Nessa Wren asks the party to find the source of a blue flame in her brass lantern. Tovin Brakes knows a three-note toll sequence: low, high, low. Let each player introduce their character and place or move their token.
2. **Blueglass Lane (10–15 min).** Orla Finch’s cart blocks the forest road. The Ash Hound is frightened and can lead the party to a safe ford. Invite the table to draw a route, then use the ruler to compare the road and ford. A forceful approach can become a short optional encounter; the hound can also be calmed.
3. **The Tollkeeper’s Vault (15–30 min).** The GM publishes the draft scene when ready. The Bell Keeper repeats the toll sequence. Answering correctly opens the inner gate. The hidden Lantern Warden guards the dais because the lantern seals a trapped river spirit. Negotiation or the sequence resolves the scene; combat is optional.

## GM setup and table exercises

- Open the separate **Amber Crossing: The Lantern Vault** campaign. **The Copper Kettle** is the published opening scene; **Blueglass Lane** and **The Tollkeeper’s Vault** are GM drafts.
- Invite players using the campaign’s normal invite flow. Keep the Vault as a draft until the table reaches it. Publishing a scene makes it available to players; hidden GM-layer notes and hidden tokens remain privileged.
- To demonstrate initiative, publish and activate the Vault, choose **Initiative**, add Mira, Kestrel, Bram, Bell Keeper, and (if revealed) Lantern Warden with any scores from your ruleset, then advance and stop the tracker. The seed deliberately leaves initiative inactive so it cannot expose future-scene names to players.
- Exercise token resources by changing Resolve or Wounds on a player character. NPC Guard and Morale are GM-only resources.
- On Blueglass Lane, draw a route or mark and measure the distance to the ford. Player drawing is enabled for this demo campaign.
- Scene switching is shared with players. Confirm the scene transition with the group before activating the next location.

## Facilitator notes (GM secrets)

The Warden is Nessa’s missing sibling, not a villain. The lantern is a seal protecting a river spirit. Tovin’s toll sequence is the low-high-low clue. Bram’s family mark is on the tollhouse door; Kestrel recognizes the blue glass as river-warden craft. The Ash Hound avoids conflict if offered food or space.

## Current limits

From the campaign list, an Owner or GM can use **Make a copy** to create an independent campaign. The copy gets new scene, layer, object, token, asset, and file IDs; it does not carry over members, chat, dice, or active initiative. Initiative must be started by the GM in the active Vault scene.
